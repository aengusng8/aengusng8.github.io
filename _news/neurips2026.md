:sparkles: <a href="/#lai2026ultraflash">UltraFlash: Accelerating Megapixel Visual Synthesis</a> was accepted at <strong class="aurora-text">NeurIPS</strong>. **Latent Hyperloop** replaces intermediate decode–resize–encode round trips with a learned latent-space shortcut, while **Sparse Patch-based Decoding** reuses cached GroupNorm statistics to reduce patch overlap. Integrated into the cascade baseline, UltraFlash delivers **approximately 2× faster 4K generation with improved image quality**.
